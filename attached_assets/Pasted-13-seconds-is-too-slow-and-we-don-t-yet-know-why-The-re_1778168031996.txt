13 seconds is too slow, and we don't yet know why
The report says "the 13s total is now dominated by the Claude API call and PodPlay I/O." That's a confident assertion without measurement to back it up. We don't actually know where those 13 seconds went. It might be Claude. It might be PodPlay. It might be something else entirely. Without a baseline measurement from before the change, we can't say what the consolidation saved.
A Claude Sonnet call typically returns in 2-4 seconds for this prompt size. PodPlay calls with our 250ms throttle on 10 events should be ~1.5 seconds (4 batches × 250ms throttle + ~200ms per call in parallel batches of 3). Total expected from those two sources: ~4-6 seconds. That leaves 7-9 seconds unaccounted for.
Possibilities:

The new player you tested has 0 attendance records, so assembleProfile returned fast — but fetchAvailablePrograms() might be slow. We've never reviewed that file.
The Anthropic call had no timeout previously and might be hitting tail latency we didn't notice.
There's another DB query somewhere in the cascade adding hundreds of ms.
Cold-start effects — first request after a server restart is always slower.

Don't guess. Measure.
