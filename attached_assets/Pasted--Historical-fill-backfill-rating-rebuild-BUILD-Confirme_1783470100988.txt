 Historical fill backfill + rating rebuild (BUILD)
Confirmed inputs: 321 past events (startTime < now, since Feb 1) resolve via GET /events/{id}, each carrying totalTeams and final signups._total.
Capacity/fill rule (final): fill_rate = signups._total / totalTeams. Do NOT use teamSize — totalTeams is the seat count already. Numerator is the API's signups._total (all registrants), NOT our attendances row count (which is a biased subset of tracked members only — see runbook note).
Step 1 — Backfill job (backfillEventFill.ts).

For each distinct event_id in attendances with attended_at >= 2026-02-01 and past startTime (~321 events): call GET /events/{id}, extract totalTeams, signups._total, startTime, status, isCanceled.
Compute fill_rate = signups._total / totalTeams. Guard against totalTeams = 0 (skip/null those, log them).
Store per event in a new event_fill table: event_id, club_id, archetype_id (via classifier), total_teams, final_signups, fill_rate, start_time, is_daily_peak, is_peak_season, is_canceled, fetched_at.
Skip canceled events (isCanceled=true) — exclude from fill averages.
Batched and resumable like existing heavy syncs: 50–100ms sleep between calls, checkpoint to job_logs, resume from last completed ID on failure. Support one-time manual invocation for the initial backfill.

Step 2 — Rebuild rating engine on historical fill.

Replace forward-looking pre-reg fill with historical fill from event_fill (completed events only — the real utilization metric).
Per archetype, sliced by daily-peak and season: mean fill_rate, with n (completed-event count) always shown.
Keep velocity and new-vs-returning but label them explicitly as tracked-member-only signals (computed over our attendances subset, not all registrants). Fill rate is the one full-population metric; do not blur.

Step 3 — Nightly upkeep.

Nightly job fetches newly-completed events not yet in event_fill and appends. Fill accrues as sessions complete. No re-fetch of recorded events.

Runbook note: attendances captures only tracked members — a variable, biased fraction of true attendance (PodPlay signups._total runs 2–6× our row count per event). Any attendance-derived metric is a member-subset signal. Fill rate must always source signups + totalTeams from the API.
