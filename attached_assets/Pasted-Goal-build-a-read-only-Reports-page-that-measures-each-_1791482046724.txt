1. Outcome view. Create a SQL view playbook_outcomes with one row per (player, playbook_key) sequence. Columns:

player_id, name, playbook_key
step1_sent_at, step2_sent_at, last_sent_at
sequence_status, exit_reason, replied (bool)
booked (bool), booked_event_id, booked_program_name, booking_created_at, credited_step (1 or 2), coupon_code_used, days_to_book
opted_out (bool), opted_out_at, optout_after_step (1 or 2), optout_message_id (the last outbound text before the STOP), hours_to_optout

Rules:

Booked means an attendance/signup whose booked_at is after step1_sent_at and on or before last_sent_at + 14 days. Make the 14 a single constant at the top of the view.
credited_step is 2 if the booking came after step2_sent_at, otherwise 1.
Exclude Learn to Play events themselves from counting as a booking.
