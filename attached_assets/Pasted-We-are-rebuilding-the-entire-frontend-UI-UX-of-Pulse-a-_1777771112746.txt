We are rebuilding the entire frontend UI/UX of Pulse — a player 
retention platform for pickleball clubs. The current UI works 
technically but feels like a developer tool. We need it to feel 
like a premium "wizard tool" — clean, intelligent, action-oriented. 
Think a mix of Gainsight's data density with Linear's visual 
cleanliness. Coaches and club owners are the users, not engineers.

THE PRODUCT GOAL:
Pulse helps club staff answer three questions every day:
1. Who needs attention and why?
2. What outreach is ready to send?
3. How is the club performing?

Every screen should make those questions easy to answer in under 
10 seconds. No jargon, no spinners with no feedback, no buttons 
that say "Run Agent."

GLOBAL UI PRINCIPLES — apply to every page:

1. LOADING STATES: Never use a plain spinner. Every async action 
   gets one of:
   - A progress bar under the button that fills over estimated time
   - A shimmer skeleton for data loading
   - A step indicator for multi-step operations ("Step 1 of 3 — 
     Fetching players...")
   Buttons show estimated time: "Send Outreach (~30s)"
   Once complete: brief success state before returning to normal

2. EMPTY STATES: Every empty list/table gets an illustrated empty 
   state with a clear action. Not just "No data."

3. MICRO-INTERACTIONS: 
   - Buttons scale slightly on hover (transform: scale(1.01))
   - Cards lift on hover (box-shadow transition)
   - Success actions show a brief green checkmark before resetting
   - Destructive actions (Dismiss) require a single confirmation 
     inline, not a modal

4. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY:
   - Page titles: 20px, fontWeight 600
   - Section headers: 13px, fontWeight 600, letterSpacing 0.04em, 
     color var(--text-secondary), UPPERCASE
   - Body: 13px, var(--text-primary)
   - Metadata/labels: 11px, var(--font-mono), var(--text-tertiary)
   - Numbers/stats: 32px, fontWeight 700, tabular-nums

5. CARD PATTERN: All panels use this base:
   background: var(--bg-surface)
   border: 1px solid var(--border)
   borderRadius: var(--radius-md)
   boxShadow: var(--shadow-sm)
   No raw sections with just a border — everything is a card.
