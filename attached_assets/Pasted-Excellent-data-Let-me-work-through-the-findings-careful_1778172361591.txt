Off-by-one bug, or the data type. daysSince is computed as:
typescript(Date.now() - new Date(lastVisitDate).getTime()) / (1000 * 60 * 60 * 24)

Issue 3: Output token sprawl on Mair (274) and Digon (622)
This is the real latency driver per your observation. Two of four runs spent meaningful tokens on reasoning before the JSON.

With richer context (Mair has lapsing complexity, Digon has buddy + already-booked complexity), the model talks itself through the decision before producing JSON. That preamble gets thrown away by the indexOf parser but we paid for the tokens and waited for them.
This is the right time to use Anthropic's tool_use structured output — it forces the model to emit JSON directly without preamble. But it's a bigger change than what we have time for in this audit. The cheap fix is to add a stronger directive to the system prompt:
OUTPUT — Your entire response must be a single JSON object and nothing else. No preamble. No reasoning before the JSON. No text after the closing brace. Begin your response with `{` and end with `}`.
