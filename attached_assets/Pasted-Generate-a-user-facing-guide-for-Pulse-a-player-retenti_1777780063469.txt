## How Pulse Works (The Big Picture)
Simple explanation of the data flow without 
technical terms:
- Pulse connects to your booking platform and learns 
  who your players are
- Every night it updates player data automatically
- It scores players by how likely they are to stop 
  coming back
- It generates personalized outreach messages for 
  at-risk players
- You review the messages, send the ones you like, 
  and track responses

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why are so many players showing as Inactive?
A: Honest answer about new clubs and December/January 
   players who tried once and drifted.

Q: A player got a message but I didn't send it — why?
A: Explain the queue — nothing sends without approval.

Q: Can Pulse send messages automatically?
A: No — every message requires staff review first.

Q: How do I know if a message worked?
A: Explain win-back rate and the return-after-message 
   tracking.

Q: What is "Level Trust" and why does it matter?
A: Plain English explanation of the confidence scoring 
   without mentioning the algorithm.

Q: A player complained they got the wrong level 
   recommendation — what do I do?
A: Assign their coach level in the player card.

Q: How often does Pulse update player data?
A: Every night automatically. You can also trigger 
   a manual refresh from the dashboard.

Q: What does "Club Friends" mean?
A: Explanation of buddy pairs in plain English.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Pulse
5-7 practical tips:
- Assess players early — the more coach levels set, 
  the smarter the recommendations
- Review the queue every morning, not just when 
  you remember
- Use the New Player Intros panel before each session
  to plan quick introductions
- Edit messages before sending — the AI is good but 
  you know your players
- Check Session Ratings weekly once Twilio is live
- Act on "High Churn Risk" players within 48 hours — 
  the window closes fast
